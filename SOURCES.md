## Assessment and explainability method

All four retained V2 check-ins use **10 original frequency-response items** on a 0–3 scale; the displayed descriptive index is `round(sum of 10 answers ÷ 30 × 100)`. The follow-up item records a contextual response and does not change the score. This software-derived score is **not a probability of disease** or validated mental-health diagnostic threshold. V3 personal-plan suggestions are a simple visible mapping from the selected assessment topic to a daily-life support focus. Mind Map links are organizational only, not empirical causal relationships, validated feature importance or model outputs.
